# The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue

Yes — the eclipse, the chanting, the sacrificial goat. All arranged. Do you know how long it takes to coordinate three hundred cultists who cannot read? I have a throne to polish, a hero to monologue at, and a raven filing complaints. The dark ritual begins at midnight, sharp, because the caterers charge extra after one. Oh, the hero will come, sword gleaming — could he not come on a Tuesday? I have a nap scheduled. Still, when the moon turns black and the ground splits open, remember who stayed up late to make it happen. Me. Always me.
