A 100-word monologue spoken by a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired. The tiredness lives in the voice, the rhythm and the small deflections — the eclipse, the chanting, the caterers, the hero who never comes on a Tuesday — while he still schemes, keeps his history, and runs the ritual on schedule. The entry is `monologue.md`.
